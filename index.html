§ 1

About

I'm a History undergraduate at University College London, working towards a career at the Bar. I'm exploring commercial, environmental and public law through mooting, competitive debating and writing on legal issues for a general readership.

Before university I spent several years in politics and media. I interned in the offices of two MPs, wrote for Newsquest, and built a political TikTok account with more than 6 million views. My podcast, Charlotte Talks Politics, has featured guests including Lord Heseltine, Lord Blunkett and Jeremy Hunt, and my interview with Alastair Campbell won Best Scoop at the National Media Awards.

That work taught me to research a subject in depth, build an argument and explain it clearly to people who aren't specialists. I'm keen to hear about mini-pupillages, legal work experience and mooting opportunities.
