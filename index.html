§ 1

About

Open with where you are now: what you are studying or doing, and the question or field that drew you to it. Keep it plain and specific; a reader should finish this paragraph knowing what you do.

Use the second paragraph for what makes you distinctive: a formative experience, a cause you work on outside your studies, or a skill you have built deliberately over time.

Close with what you are looking for next, such as a placement, a role, a collaboration or a conversation, so the reader knows how they can help.
